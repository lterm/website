About

I shoot film, in 35mm and medium format, mostly in black and white: strong contrasts, deep shadows, silhouettes and graphic architecture.

Mountains
From the GR20 to Bessans, from Chamonix to the Pyrenees: trails, snowy ridges, winter.
Coastline
The Côte d’Opale, rocky shores and their lighthouse, sea horizons reduced to a single line.
Cities
New York, Boston, London, Paris. Street photography, in passing.
Presences
Dancing bodies lit against black. Concerts, a piano. Close ones, interiors, the light from a window.
Dates
Some images carry their moment: Paris empty and masked during lockdown, the Arc de Triomphe wrapped by Christo in 2021.
Colour and experiments
Colour comes with the Lomo cameras and the Olympus XA4. Elsewhere, experimentation takes over: bluish cross-processing, light leaks, shadows turning abstract.
